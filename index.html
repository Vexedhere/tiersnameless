GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

The details before you make the move.

How does the first-month-free offer work?

The offer is highlighted on paid plans. The current checkout system still uses manual UPI payment verification and does not yet have automated trial activation or expiry. Contact the Nameless Labs team before submitting payment if you want to redeem the ₹0 first-month promotion; do not pay the displayed monthly amount expecting an automatic free trial.

What exactly is the 3-hour image pool?

Your plan includes a rolling image-generation allowance: 5 for Free, 20 for Base, and 100 for Plus or Pro. As generations age out of the 3-hour window, that capacity becomes available again. The pool is not a daily quota.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime?

You can request a tier change at any time. Paid upgrades are submitted through the UPI checkout flow and become active after an administrator verifies the transaction. Since billing is manually reviewed, cancellation and refunds are handled by the Nameless Labs team.

Can I pay annually and get the 20% discount?

The annual option is shown as a pricing preview. Annual payment collection is not enabled in the current manual UPI workflow yet; checkout currently accepts monthly plan submissions only.

How do I know which plan I'm currently on?

Sign in with your Nameless Labs account. This page reads your membership tier from your Supabase profile and marks your active plan.
